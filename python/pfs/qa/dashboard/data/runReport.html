Science frames: offsets from the calibration

The x offset is the median offset of every fiber's trace from the calibration. The y offset is that of the sky lines, taking the species furthest off on each image. The km/s value converts each species' offset with the dispersion at its own lines. Values are as measured: on visits read with the m arm the gate first subtracts a standing offset (the b arm's in x, the n arm's in y) where qadb holds one, so a value drawn here may sit beyond a limit it passed.

How each kind is judged

Every verdict on this page is the one drp_qa's gate stored in qadb for the image; the page judges nothing itself. The gate's checks today, each WARN then FAIL (a value at a limit has crossed it):

    {{CHECKS}}
  • A visit takes the worst verdict among its detectors. UNKNOWN means the QA ran and nothing could be judged, or qadb holds no verdict for the image. A verdict stored before a check was changed is as it was judged then.
    • {{NO_RESULT_NOTE}}
  • Not covered: visits after the latest one judged, whose results may be on their way; and darks, biases and master frames, which have no QA method. Whether a flagged frame is truly faulty has not been checked.

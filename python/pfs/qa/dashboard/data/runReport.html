How each kind is judged

  • Science: the offset of traces (x) and sky lines (y) from the calibration, {{OFFSET_RULE}}; and the sky-line residual after the visit's detectorMap adjustment, {{KMS_RULE}}. Width and line scatter are not judged on science frames.
  • All-sky: the offsets from the calibration, WARN at 1 px and FAIL at 2 px.
  • Arcs and quartz, full slit: width, line or trace scatter and lines per fiber, each against the detector's own reference; and the offsets from the calibration.
  • Twilights: the offsets from the calibration, and the consistency of each exposure with the rest of its sequence.
  • Engineering-fiber frames: width and lines per fiber against their own references, the trace offset, and line residuals. Their line residuals are compared with full-slit references, which were not made for these frames; most of their FAILs come from that comparison and from the trace offset.
  • A visit takes the worst verdict among its detectors. UNKNOWN means the QA ran and nothing could be judged.
    • {{NO_RESULT_NOTE}}
  • Not covered: visits after the latest one judged, whose results may be on their way; and darks, biases and master frames, which have no QA method. Whether a flagged frame is truly faulty has not been checked.
